# Business-Plan-2025
This is a repository dedicated to my creating a thorough business plan for a ficticious company that would be based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

##Business in a Nutshell

The business, named Store2Door, is a service based business that would be based in Johannesburg, South Africa. It's main offices would be based in Midrand, for reasons that are laid out in the business plan.

The business provides a service by being the middleman between business and clientele. It acts as a method of delivery of items, or people from 1 place to another.
